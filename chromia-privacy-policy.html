Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Chromia ("the Game") is developed and published by HyperCube Studio ("we", "our", or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Chromia does not require user registration and does not directly collect personally identifiable information.

However, the Game uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain information, including but not limited to:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information (device model, operating system)
  • IP address (used for approximate location)
  • App interaction data
  • Diagnostics and crash data

This information may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized ads, improve ad performance, and prevent fraud and abuse.

All data collected through AdMob is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Locally Stored Data

The Game stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • High scores for each game mode
  • User preferences (vibration and sound settings)

This data is stored using the device's local storage and is never transmitted to any server or third party. You can clear this data at any time by using the "Reset All Highscores" option in the Settings screen or by uninstalling the Game.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data directly. Any data collected through advertising services is handled by the respective third-party provider (Google).

5. Children's Privacy

Chromia is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

6. Data Security

Any data transmitted through third-party services is encrypted in transit using secure technologies. All local game data remains on your device and is not transmitted externally.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at:
studio@hypercube.tr
